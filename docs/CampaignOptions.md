# Campaign deck selection

With Campaign enabled, open **Help & Options > Plugins > Custom Opponent Decks** to switch the additional opponent-deck picker on or off.

* **On** (the default): Campaign's Custom Decks choice asks for your deck, then your opponent's deck. Normal Free Duel also asks for the opponent's deck after yours.
* **Off**: Campaign uses the original Story Deck / User Deck menu, and normal Free Duel proceeds after choosing your deck. The opponent uses their normal deck.

The setting takes effect immediately and is retained between launches in `Yu-Gi-Oh-Ex\campaign-options.json` (an old one beside the exe is moved there once). A failed write keeps the previous setting. No Config.ini editing is needed.

Tag Duel retains its required deck-selection steps for its multiple seats. Character, deck, story and tutorial content supplied by Campaign continues to load with either setting.
